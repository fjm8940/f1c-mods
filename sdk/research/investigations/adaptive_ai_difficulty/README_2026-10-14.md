# Adaptive AI difficulty — orientation note (2026-10-14)

Owner question: what RaceAbility and AI Strength do, what sets AI lap times, and
whether an auto-adjusting AI difficulty mod is feasible (friend wins with BAR at strength 93).
Stage: investigate only. No code, game or settings changed.

## Verified (from existing corpus, not re-tested today)
- Paper 41 / findings `rcd-offsets-live-confirmed`, `0x44F4-pace-scalar` (FACT):
  per-driver baseline pace `veh+0x44F4 = (1 - RaceAbility/100) * GDS`. Lower RA = faster.
  Pace factor 0x453C walks +/-0.01 per frame toward 0x44F4 (or 0x44F4+boost when attacking).
  0x44F4 is never rewritten in session, so a live poke persists (poke-proven).
- Paper 42: AI Driver Strength (70-120) -> GDS interpolated from track .AIW
  WorstAdjust/MidAdjust/BestAdjust (live: Monza s=92 -> 0.96). ~0.4-0.5 s/lap per click.
  AI Realism = aggression only, no lap-time path.
- `AIDifficultyScalar` 0x424750: strength>=95 -> 1.0, else 0.75+0.01*(s-70); throttle cap 0x457C.
- `qualifyingability-simulated-sessions-only-2026-10-02`: QualifyingAbility only affects
  simulated (skipped-session) times; driven qualifying uses RaceAbility path.

## Inference: what decides AI lap time
AI lap = car physics (team .car/.hdv/engine, shared with player model) x driver pace
scalar (RA x GDS) bounded by PLR caps (AI Corner Grip/Brake Grip/Brake Power Usage).
With all RA equal the driver term is identical, so order = car performance —
matches owner observation (RA=1 qualifying 1:1 with engine performance).
