Generated by `phase2/analysis/c5_write_readiness.py`. NOTHING here has been written to
the game. Persistence classes come from `persistence_map.jsonl` (lab probe, slot 1,
one write then next-tick read; `LATCHED` = persisted, `RECALCULATED` = game rewrote it,
`VOLATILE*` = changed during the probe, `SKIPPED_POINTER/PROTECTED/READ_FAILED` = no
probe value). Stage numbers are doc 54 section 1: 1 heading, 2 waypoint locator,
3 pre-traffic (AIAbility/AIPushLap/AIStateFlagSetter), 4 opponent scan, 5 AIRacingLine,
6 AISteering, 7 steering law, 8 AISteeringCorrection, 9 gear logic, 10 mode switch,
11 output copy. Writers per field: `phase2/WRITER_CENSUS.csv`.

Rule of thumb: a write sticks only if it happens after the field's last in-tick
writer and survives to the consumer. Fields the game RECALCULATES need a per-tick
hook; LATCHED fields can also be held from outside. A post-hook on AIMainUpdate
0x424E70 (the mechanical_failures base) overrides outputs for one tick.
