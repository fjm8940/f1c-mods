## Method and verification

- The dossier `literal_hits` grep found the literal eid hits. Their lines were classified. Many are non-eid (switch case labels, struct offsets, vtable slots).
- 40 functions call `DriverDataTableLookupAndTimeConversion` (grep over `functions/*.c`). These are the complete emitter set. The 18 dynamic-eid functions were read in full, along with all literal emitters for the batch eids.
- Name and track lookup functions (DriverNameIDLookup, NameListBuilderFromVehicleArray, TrackNameDisplayIDLookup, sub_417640, sub_414A90 path) store eids into name or track structs. None of them calls DriverDataTable. Their callers do not pass those fields to an emitter.
- Each row's `checked` list covers the literal hits, all 18 dynamic functions, and the lookup hits (20 to 36 entries per row).

## Caveats (why most NATIVE_ACTIVE rows are DERIVED)

1. Writers of car sector times (car+16460) and the pole reference table (flt_5F0780) are not located in `functions/`. Only reads are found. sub_49BE50 (HUD gap) reads the same arrays, so the game does populate them. Live calibration needed for eids 13, 14, 19, 20.
2. `veh+0x4060` (race position) was measured in RACE only (finding `f1c-race-position-is-4060-not-32d0`). Its use in qualifying for eids 17 and 18 is not measured.

4. Gate `a2[4111]` (eid 17 at line 25; eid 21 counter) is the same field as `car+16444`. Its meaning is not established. Live check needed.
