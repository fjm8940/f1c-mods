## Key findings (fact vs inference)

- FACT (code): eid 126 (GREEN01) has one native emitter, `PitSelectionStateMachine` (sub_41B3C0) line 72,
  in its flag CLEAR branch. Line 36 blocks it when `dword_7C56A4 != 7 || dword_7C56A8 == 5`.
- FACT (code): `sub_4D4580` runs the flag dispatcher only in race phases 5-8 (lines 43-115). Phase 8 is
  blocked at dispatcher line 11. So GREEN01 can play only in phase 6 or 7.
- UNKNOWN: whether a vanilla race ever enters phase 6 or 7. The writes to `dword_7C56A8` are indirect; no
  visible writer sets 6 or 7. This is the blocker for GREEN01.
- FACT (code): the red-flag branch (case 3, line 151, eid 131) is fed only by zone state 3. The local
  writer `sub_4D1730` writes 0 (line 27) and 2 (line 71). State 3 can only come from a received flag
  record (`sub_4B3400` case 4 -> `sub_4D1A50` at line 221). No local producer found.
- FACT (code): OIL 127-129 and MECHANWARN 130 have no literal or computed emitter in the 40 functions.
  None of their values appears as a constant in any emitter.
- FACT (code): SLOWCAR01 PWS 132 has no emitter. The TV twin 427 is emitted only in
  `ScoringClassificationDisplay` (line 129), a post-race standings function.
- DERIVED: the SC clips have no PWS eid. Their only native TV eid candidates are 423 (constructor
  standings, no static caller) and none for 425/428.
- DERIVED: the owner-game mods come from `LightloaderDisabled.ini`: `redflag.dll=1`, `safetycar.dll=1`
  (disabled), `adaptive_ai_difficulty.dll=1`. Mechanical Failures 1.0.7 engine family is active (brief).
