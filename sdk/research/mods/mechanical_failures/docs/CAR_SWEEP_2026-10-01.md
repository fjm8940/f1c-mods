# Car sweep 2026-10-01 - how the engine model fares on every installed car

Model source: E9b-1 snapshot (`workspace_organization_audit/ENGINE_SIM_BEFORE_2026-09-30_E9b-2`, model 9EE23EF3), not
DeepSeek's in-progress files. Harness: `tools/car_sweep/`. 145 engine files (every .ini with an RPMTorque table).
Grouping "vanilla-era 99-02" = folders 1999_-2002_ (the seasons F1C ships); the files may still be modded (inference).
Evidence tier: synthetic simulation (TEST), not live. Profiles are harsh: RACE sits on the limiter ~2/3 of each lap.

## Results
- Car data: 136/145 files derive with NO fallback. Fallbacks: 7 mod files from 1994-95 (Ford HB / Ilmor, Forti,
  Pacific) have no torque-zero crossing in the table -> torque-zero fallback 1.15 x limit (fine); FIA safety-car
  variants (sci101, restrictor) lack lifetime keys (not a player car).
- Manual faults are car-independent: oil pressure (medium) bearings gone after 183-202 s at RACE on all 145 files;
  valve (medium) 180 s on all. Redistributable.
- Natural failures follow each car's own engine.ini life law (faithful to the modder):
  | era | files | drain at limiter | RACE end (median) | ABUSE end |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1995-2001, 2003RH | ~10-16 each | x2.2-2.3 | 110-120 min | ~21 min |
  | 1994 mods | 14 | x5.8 | 45 min | 10 min |
  | 2002 (vanilla) | 12 | x2.3 | 36 min (LifetimeAvg ~55 min) | 6 min |
  | 2004RH / 2005RH mods | 20 | x12.7-16 | 13-15 min | see bug |
  GENTLE survives 3 h on every car. Real driving shifts before the limiter (live F2002: life ~4.7 h), so these
  are worst cases, useful only relative to each other.
- 2004RH/2005RH mods use `LifetimeEngineRPM` range 150 rpm and a limit 750 rpm above base: the game itself
  punishes the limiter hard there. The mod inherits that (by design: the game clock is WHEN).

## BUG found (E9g-c reset rule vs steep life curves)
2004RH/2005RH cars at ABUSE/CASUAL: life drops from thousands to 0 within seconds; the last drop is larger than
the reset floor `max(5, 3 x drain_ema x dt + 1)` because drain_ema lags -> the model logs "life reset (not a
failure)" instead of a culmination. Effect in game: the game kills the engine itself, but the mod misses its
presentation (no radio/HUD, no dead latch after E9g-f). Same happened 1x at RACE (94renaultv10).
Fix proposal (batch E9g-g): floor from the larger of drain_ema and the instantaneous game law
`mf_game_drain(car, rpm, oil, stress)` for the sample; add a test with a 150 rpm range car.

## Verdict
Redistributable: derivation robust on 94 % of files, fallbacks sane on the rest, manual faults identical on every
car. One bug for steep-curve mods (above). Next: re-run after DeepSeek's final run (E9b-2 changes timing).

## Engine grading analysis (owner question, 2026-10-01)
Season data (engine.ini, TEST/file read):
- 1995 (mod): LifetimeAvg 167-176 min for all 13 cars, LifetimeVar ~56-60 min; values grouped by engine supplier
  (same base/range per supplier). The modder did not grade reliability: the field is flat.
- 2001 (vanilla-era): LifetimeAvg 132 (Arrows) - 173 min (Benetton), Var 15 (Prost) - 57 min; top teams ~170,
  backmarkers ~133. The game data already grades teams.
Consequence: the game's own LifetimeAvg/Var already grades WHEN each engine dies. The E9b-2 quality q (random per
engine 0.85-1.15) is build-to-build variance on top. The E9p "team quality by team name" would need a hand-made
rating
table (not redistributable) and double-counts LifetimeAvg.
Proposal (post-run batch): grade G = this engine's starting life / median starting life of the field in the same
session (read from memory, works with any mod; player 0x7C91DC, AI veh+0x3D70), clamped 0.8-1.2, applied only to HOW
(fault growth x G^-0.5, early hazard x G^-2, niggles x 1/G), never to the clock. Flat mod fields -> G ~1 for all.
Correction (Claude, same night, tested on the real files): the drain/headroom term is dropped. With it, low-revving
1995 Ford customer engines graded 1.20 (wrong). Final formula (E9q): score = 0.7 ln(LifetimeAvg) + 0.3 ln(max(Avg -
Var, 0.25 Avg)), G = exp(score - field median) clamped 0.8-1.2. Results: 2001 F2001 1.07, B201 1.04, BAR003 0.87,
A22 0.82, EJ11 0.82; 1995 all 0.96-1.03; 1994 Ford customer engines 0.80 vs works 1.00; 2002 F2002 1.20, BAR004 0.86;
2004RH F2004 1.06 ... PS04 0.93. Limit: a mod that gives every team the same LifetimeAvg/Var grades flat (no other
reliability data exists in the files); optional mf_grades.ini override is the escape hatch.
