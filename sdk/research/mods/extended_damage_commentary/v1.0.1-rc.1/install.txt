Extended Damage Commentary 1.0.1 (free mod)

Your race engineer comments when your car loses a front wing, rear wing or a wheel.

Easiest: open Lightloader Manager, go to Mod catalog, tick Extended Damage
Commentary and press Install.

By hand:
1. Close the game.
2. Copy extended_damage_commentary.dll into the game's plugins\lightloader folder.
3. Start the game.

To remove it, delete extended_damage_commentary.dll from plugins\lightloader.

Bug reports: https://mods.palloset.com/report/
